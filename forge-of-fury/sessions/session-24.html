Voice 2 · Player recap

Player-provided · Thrain’s notebook 10/26/26

We rest at the winery and are ready for the day. Lard had some time to read over the tome and shared some interesting insights into Strahd. Davin Martikov and his raven have interests in keeping the party safe. He accompanied us on our trek to the tower where we hope to find an advanced vampire slayer and a place to keep Ireena safe. The exit from the winery is through a cutout area of a very dense forest. After 2-3hr of walking, we arrive at the tower. The tower’s door is garnished with a sigil. Even Davin is confused by it. Qiran inspects the area around the tower. Everything feels abandoned. Thrain knocked on the door and tried to push it open. The sigil is a lock spell. Guess nobody’s home.

Suddenly, Rudolph van Richten answered the door and hurried us into the tower, as he said “before we were seen”. We introduce ourselves and reveal the party’s story. We talk about the tarroka card reading that we were given before we were swept away by the fog.

Dr. Richten gives us his interpretation and a better understanding of the Druid card, the Rogue, Wizard, Raven, and the Ghost cards and why we might have been brought to these lands. Other topics were discussed giving the party more interesting information on what we might face. Benn acquired a ‘the sunsword’. It is a radiant beam of light that will cause immense damage to vampires when hit with it. Farmer Benn will need time to train with this weapon, as he is not accustomed to the style of fighting he will need to use. He better learn fast as the party now knows Strahd can sense their location.
