Ironvein Smithy · Steel for the mine. Iron for the road. Between sessions — not Deep Memory.

Ironvein Smithy

Welcome

Heat rolls through the doorway before you cross the threshold. Hammer blows ring from the rear forge, sparks jump against black stone, and Durnan Ironvein looks up only after finishing the strike already in motion.

Most of the floor is mining gear — picks, chisels, reinforced barrows. Weapons and shields hang ready too; the finer work is commission, not parade.

Steel for the mine. Iron for the road. Better work for those who know the difference.

Durnan: “Pretty costs extra. Strong doesn’t.”

Smith

Durnan Ironvein — squat, formidable, more interested in the next strike than small talk.

Gimble sells possibilities. Durnan sells things that will still work after something tries to kill you.

1 · Ready from the Rack

Practical metal for mine and road — picks, fittings, and everyday steel for Blasingdell and the trail.

Player’s Handbook items are also available: standard weapons, armor, shields, ammunition, and common adventuring gear at the usual prices. Ask Durnan (or check the book) — no need to list every dagger on the wall.
Featured this downtime: Silvered Longsword on the rack — ready work, not parade steel. Ask Durnan for the price of the silvering on top of the blade.

2 · Forged & Found

Magic steel for Tempest on the road — +1 work only. Uncommon on the rack; rare pieces stay few. Not a very-rare vault.

Uncommon · five on the rack

  • +1 Battleaxe uncommon

    +1 to attack and damage. Mine-road steel with a bit of true temper.

  • +1 Longsword uncommon

    +1 to attack and damage. Clean lines; no fancy name needed.

  • +1 Warhammer uncommon

    +1 to attack and damage. Durnan’s favorite shape of argument.

  • +1 Morningstar uncommon

    +1 to attack and damage. For when the mountain still has undead in it.

  • +1 Shield uncommon

    +1 to AC (with the shield’s usual bonus). Holds when pretty paint would peel.

Rare · unique steel (not just plus)

Enough odd pieces for fine adventurers to talk about — prices at the table / ask Durnan (DM the DM).

  • Flame Tongue rare

    A sword that can burst into flame on command — forge-fire that answers the wielder.

  • Giant Slayer rare

    A weapon tempered to hurt giants (and giant-kin) more than ordinary steel.

  • Dragon Slayer rare

    A weapon made for killing dragons. Nightscale’s name has been muttered near this rack.

  • Frost Brand rare

    Cold steel from a hot shop — a sword that bites with frost and shrugs fire.

  • Sword of Wounding rare

    Wounds that keep bleeding. Ugly work. Durnan respects it.

  • Animated Shield rare

    A shield that can guard you on its own while your hands stay free.

  • Arrow-Catching Shield rare

    Pulls ranged attacks toward it — and can snatch a missile out of the air.

  • Spellguard Shield rare

    Shield bonus against spells and advantage on saves against them.

  • Armor of Resistance rare

    Resistance to one damage type (chosen when found — e.g. fire, necrotic). Ask which suit is on the stand.

  • Elven Chain rare

    Mithral chain that doesn’t announce itself. Rare mail that still feels like real armor.

Durnan on enchantment: He can add a +1 to a suitable weapon, shield, or armor — 350–500 gp, time and deposit at the table. Turning a +1 into a +2 costs a lot more — commission only, not walk-out stock, and not for every piece of steel that walks in. “Pretty costs extra. Strong doesn’t. True temper costs what it costs.”

3 · Repairs & Commission Work

Durnan repairs weapons, shields, and armor; replaces straps and fittings; silvers steel; and takes custom commissions (shields, weapons, dwarven detailing) with a deposit and a wait. Strength and balance first — decoration costs more.

At the table: prices depend on damage, materials, and how soon you need it back. Ask Durnan (or the DM). “Pretty costs extra. Strong doesn’t.”

4 · Durnan Will Buy

Useful metalwork, salvage, and gear he can restore or resell. Final prices stay a table call.

Usually accepted

  • Martial and simple weapons
  • Shields
  • Medium and heavy armor
  • Metal ammunition
  • Mining tools
  • Usable metal salvage
  • Ingots and workable ore
  • Damaged gear with recoverable parts

Requires appraisal

  • Dwarven weapons or armor
  • Unusual alloys
  • Durgeddin-marked pieces
  • Magical weapons, armor, or shields
  • Badly damaged relics
  • Items of uncertain origin

Usually refused

  • Cloth or leather goods
  • Ordinary jewelry
  • Arcane curios
  • Badly corroded scrap with no workable metal
  • Stolen goods that are obviously identifiable

Durnan pays according to condition, usefulness, and how much work it will take to put an item back on the rack.
