About the Adventure

Descent into Avernus is a story of damnation and defiance: holy cities, devil bargains, and the cost of salvation when the sky itself turns wrong. This table plays that module as On the Rise — starting in the living world of the Western Heartlands before the road leads somewhere darker.

Expect moral gray, hard choices, and moments of courage under impossible light. What happens after Session Zero is for the table to discover together.

Baldur’s Gate

Baldur’s Gate — crowded docks, tall walls, and ambition that never quite sleeps. Trade and travel move through here; so do secrets. For this company, the Gate is where the story’s mortal footing begins — before rivers, politics, and rumor pull the gaze east along the Chionthar.

Elturel

“Beneath the light of the Companion, Elturel stands as a beacon of hope, courage, and unwavering faith, linked to Baldur’s Gate by the River Chionthar, trade, travel, and the politics of the Western Heartlands.”
