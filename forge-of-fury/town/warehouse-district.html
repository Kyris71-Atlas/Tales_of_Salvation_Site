Warehouse District

Welcome to GrifterWare

Heat off the canal, chalk dust on crate lids, and someone yelling “GW!” like it’s both a greeting and a receipt. Tempest Company already made an impression here — shirts sold, scarves worn, tonics tasted, and one very public sword display that the stalls have not forgotten.

Gold and final trades live at the table / Fantasy Grounds. This board is what the district is pushing this downtime.

Down by the waterline, the Dock Boys are watching — lower edge of the map, male tokens on the boards, not in the deal… yet.

1 · Faces of the District

Names Tempest already knows from the floor.

Kell “Needle” Voss

Sharp trader · deal closer

Talks fast, cuts cleaner. Learn all about the cost of Friendship with Needle.

Mara “Quickcoin” Vale

Weights · ledgers · interesting jewelry

Thrilled she had the chance to meet Tempest Company First Hand. Now she’s chalking a new board: Dragon Slayer merchandise, coming soon.

Dex “Dead Heat” Marr

Odds booth · bets taken, results debated

Mask, mouth, and a book that never quite balances. If there’s a wager on the district floor, Dead Heat is probably holding the chalk.

2 · Tempest Styles

Tempest bought a T-shirt, a scarf, and a tonic off the rack — not the first of each, and not the last. The shelf was restocked during the purchase. New and Approved.

  • “Lard Lifts the Mountain” shirt Approved ask stall

    Lard bought one. The stall put three more up before he’d finished flexing.

  • Qiran-themed scarf Approved ask stall

    Benn wore his. The rack never went empty.

  • Farmer Benn Recovery Tonic Approved ask stall

    Benn bought a bottle. Another bottle appeared. The label still promises recovery; the shelf promises inventory.

3 · The Odds Booth

Dex “Dead Heat” Marr takes the action. Chalk lines change with the crowd — place your coin, argue the terms.

  • Nightscale still breathing by Days End? long odds

    Dead Heat’s favorite line. Pays loud if the mountain stays hungry.

  • How many times does Lard flex before sundown? over / under

    The shirt alone moved the line. Reprints don’t count as flexes. Probably.

  • “Curse that Scarf” — how many have been sold? guess the jar

    Closest number without going over. The Qiran scarves are the top seller on the wall — everyone sees them, nobody agrees on the count. Dead Heat keeps the tally under the crate.

  • Open book name your stakes

    Anything else the chalk can hold. Dead Heat keeps the slate; the district keeps the opinions.

4 · Mara’s Counter

Jewelry, ledgers, and whatever just became fashionable because an adventurer wouldn’t shut up about it.

Coming Soon — Dragon Slayer merch

Knockoff steel, souvenir tags, and rack prices chalked for tourists. Not the blade Tempest carries — the idea of the blade, boxed for impulse buys. Ask Mara (or the DM) when the first crate lands.

5 · GrifterWare Catalog

Wider junk wall — shirts, scarves, dubious tonics, and crate-specials. Prices flex with charm, bulk, and whether Needle is watching.

  • District shirts & scarves ask stall

    Tempest faces optional. Taste optional.

  • Recovery tonics & “cures” ask stall

    Sister Maerith’s shelf is safer. This shelf is louder.

  • Crate specials varies

    Labeled vaguely. Sold confidently. Identify at your own risk — or walk to Gimble.

Special requests

  • Trades and “found” goods
  • Rush reprints of Tempest Styles
  • Odds and side bets (Dead Heat)
  • Whatever Mara’s ledger can justify before sundown
  • Whatever Needle’s people can “find” before the Dock Boys notice

Deposit, wait time, and whether Needle’s people like you — settled at the table. The Dock Boys are still just watching from the waterline.
