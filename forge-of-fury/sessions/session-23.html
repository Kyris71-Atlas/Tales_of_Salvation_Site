Session 23

10/19/26 · Ravenloft’s Fury · Tempest Company · Wizard of Wines · assault repelled

Tempest Company — two voices. Voice 1 — table notes (session record). Voice 2 — player-provided recap.

Voice 1 · Table notes

From session record · 10/19/26 · table-safe

Worn from Yester Hill — then the vineyard hits the winery. The house holds. Road turns to Van Richten’s Tower.

Opening blows

  • Benn Turn Undead — both vampire spawn resist.
  • Mantis Crown of Madness on the Vistani woman leading the assault; enemy coordination frays.
  • Rik claws Benn, misses Mantis, grapples Lard; Thrain knocks Rik prone; Mantis Shocking Grasp — Rik to dust.

Roof & Ireena

  • Qiran casts Fly on Ireena, Misty Steps to the roof; Vistani caster follows; Arosa flies up in raven form.
  • Mre’ssa rushes Benn to bite — Natural 1. No bite.
  • Sir Kluth summoned; enemy caster answers with Greater Invisibility and Misty Step.

Retreat

  • Zorg killed while fleeing; remaining attackers withdraw.
  • Company entered spent and left with surprisingly little additional damage.
  • Benn’s parting shout: “And don’t come back!”

State

  • Wizard of Wines held; Ireena still with the defenders; Arosa fought openly.
  • Next destination: Van Richten’s Tower — Ireena wants the legendary hunter’s protection.

Notes expand when more is thrown at Atlas. Same session as the player recap — different job.

Voice 2 · Player recap

Player-provided · Thrain’s notebook 10/19/26

Initiative rolls. Crown of madness charms Manusa, a vampire. We all attack. A Vastani woman tries to fear us and she does frighten most of the party. And then that effect ended almost as fast as it casted. Qiran goes invisible and saves Ireena; casting fly on her. They both land on the rooftop. Manusa shakes off the madness effect. Vampires claw and bite. Werewolves claw and bite. The party deals damage. Benn’s radiant words hurt. Kluth joins the party and attacks Zora. Zora disappeared. Werewolves run away. The last vampire also tries to flee, and succeeds. Until Lard…. chases and keeps attacking to its death. We are thanked for the help and saving Ireena.
