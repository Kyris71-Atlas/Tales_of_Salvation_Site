Dawnbreaker Claymore

Damien’s legacy weapon · Faith · Lathander
Legacy weapon On the Rise Damien Lightshadow
Awakening Faith — first spark Form Greatsword / claymore · heavy · two-handed Play sheet Damien · CCS
What it is

A massive two-handed claymore forged from silver celestial alloy. The crossguard resembles the wings of a rising phoenix, a symbol of Lathander’s renewal. Runes along the blade grow more responsive as its legacy awakens.

How It Answered Damien

The Order of the Aster had seen the contradiction within him: Zariel’s blood carried a legacy of wrath, while he had chosen to place that fire in the service of the Dawn. When Damien became a Paladin, the runes along Dawnbreaker stirred for the first time in 150 years. A faint, warm golden light traced the blade. The sword did not ask the Paladin to extinguish the fire within him. It agreed with where he had chosen it would burn.

Dawn Sign

Light of the Morning. The runes normally shed dim light in a 5-foot radius (faint warm golden glow). When Damien casts a spell or uses a magical Paladin feature, they flare to bright light 10 feet and dim light for an additional 10 feet, until the start of his next turn.

At the table

Live numbers and toggles stay on Damien’s Company Character Sheet. This page is the blade’s dossier — story, stage, and what the dawn means when he carries it.

Design north star

Dawnbreaker grows because Damien grows. It is becoming fully itself.
