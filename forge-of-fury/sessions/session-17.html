Voice 2 · Player recap

Player-provided · Thrain's notebook 8/17/26

Rest and recovery begins this session’s start. We met with The Lady and provided our mapped areas and compensated accordingly. We agreed that the dragon was our next goal. Onto our quest to gear up. We’ve uncovered that Nightscale can attack with acid. Not good! The Lady has a deal with the vendors to pay half price for items purchased. A contract is required to be filled out, that is more than it initially seems. Lard and Thrain have signed but is the cost is going to be more than what they’re willing to pay? A visit to the Warehouse District reveals the excitement around the Tempest. We also are impressed with the creativity and make a few purchases of our own. The Dragonslayer and a ring of acid resistance have been added to the party with the hopes of potions and upgraded gear within the next few days before heading back out.
