Combat Magic Helm

Helm spells use DC 18 (item) · not Sol’s warlock DC 21

Item Helm of Brilliance Very rare · attuned · worn Gem spell DC 18 Page 3 of 3 · room for spell text

Combat keeps attack, AC, gems, and flame toggle. Magic keeps pact spells and invocations. This page is the Helm of Brilliance spell book — full text so Combat stays playable.

1 · Helm at a glance

Helm of Brilliance
Very rare · attuned · worn · gem-spell save DC 18

While gems remain: ≥1 ruby → resistance to fire; ≥1 diamond → dim light 30 ft; undead that start turn in that light take 1d6 radiant; ≥1 fire opal → action + command: weapon you hold blazes (+1d6 fire on hit; bright 10 / dim +10). Toggle with Flame; bonus action to douse / drop ends it.

Gems remaining — shared with Combat (same device / browser). Decrement when you cast.

Risk: if you take fire damage from failing a spell save while wearing the helm, roll d20; on a 1 the remaining gems flash outward (each creature within 60 ft: DC 17 DEX save, radiant damage = number of gems left, half on success) and the helm is destroyed.

2 · Gem spells (action · consumes 1 gem · DC 18)

Cast as an action using the listed gem as the material component (the gem is destroyed). These are item spells — save DC 18, not Sol’s warlock DC 21.

Daylight Opal

3rd-level evocation · Action · Range 60 ft · V S M (opal) · Duration 1 hour

A 60-foot-radius sphere of light spreads out from a point you choose within range. The sphere is bright light and sheds dim light for an additional 60 feet.

If you chose a point on an object you are holding or one that isn’t worn or carried, the light shines from the object and moves with it. Completely covering the object with something opaque blocks the light.

If any of this spell’s area overlaps with an area of darkness created by a spell of 3rd level or lower, the spell that created the darkness is dispelled.

Fireball Fire opal

3rd-level evocation · Action · Range 150 ft · V S M (fire opal) · Instantaneous · DEX save DC 18

A bright streak flashes from you to a point you choose within range and then blossoms with a low roar into an explosion of flame. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a Dexterity saving throw.

Fail: 8d6 fire damage.
Success: half damage.

The fire spreads around corners. It ignites flammable objects in the area that aren’t being worn or carried.

At higher levels the spell deals more dice — the helm casts it as listed (base fireball).

Wall of Fire Ruby

4th-level evocation · Action · Range 120 ft · V S M (ruby) · Concentration, up to 1 minute · DEX save DC 18

You create a wall of fire on a solid surface within range. You can make the wall up to 60 feet long, 20 feet high, and 1 foot thick, or a ringed wall up to 20 feet in diameter, 20 feet high, and 1 foot thick. The wall is opaque and lasts for the duration.

When the wall appears, each creature within its area must make a Dexterity saving throw. Fail: 5d8 fire. Success: half.

One side of the wall (chosen when you cast) deals 5d8 fire to each creature that ends its turn within 10 feet of that side or inside the wall. A creature takes the same damage when it enters the wall for the first time on a turn or ends its turn there. The other side of the wall deals no damage.

Concentration — track on Combat or Magic concentration line if needed.

Prismatic Spray Diamond

7th-level evocation · Action · Self (60-ft cone) · V S · Instantaneous · DEX save DC 18

Eight multicolored rays of light flash from your hand. Each ray is a different color and has a different power and purpose. Each creature in a 60-foot cone must make a Dexterity saving throw. For each target, roll a d8 to determine which color ray affects it.

  • 1 · Red: 10d6 fire (half on success).
  • 2 · Orange: 10d6 acid (half on success).
  • 3 · Yellow: 10d6 lightning (half on success).
  • 4 · Green: 10d6 poison (half on success).
  • 5 · Blue: 10d6 cold (half on success).
  • 6 · Indigo: Restrained. Then CON save end of each turn: fail 3× → petrified; succeed → ends. On a 1 on the d20 for that save, also becomes petrified (per spell). Table: restrained on fail; CON saves to end.
  • 7 · Violet: Blinded. Then WIS save start of next turn: fail → transported to another plane (banished-style); success → blindness ends.
  • 8 · Special: Target is struck by two rays. Roll twice more, rerolling any 8.

Full indigo/violet edge cases: DMG / PHB spell text if a corner case comes up mid-table.
