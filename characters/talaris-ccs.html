What you can spend on your turn — same spirit as Fantasy Grounds powers.

Sneak Attack
Once per turn · +3d6 · finesse or ranged

Deal extra 3d6 when you hit with advantage, or when an ally is within 5 ft of the target and you don’t have disadvantage.

Cunning Action
Bonus action

Dash, Disengage, or Hide.

Assassinate
Assassin 3 · surprise / first round

Advantage on attack rolls against any creature that hasn’t taken a turn in combat yet. Any hit you score against a surprised creature is a critical hit.

Uncanny Dodge
Reaction · when an attacker you can see hits you

Halve the attack’s damage against you.

Ring of Evasion
Attuned · Reaction · when you fail a DEX save · 3 charges · regain 1d3 at dawn

Spend 1 charge to succeed on the failed Dexterity saving throw instead.

Dancing Lights
High Elf cantrip · CHA

Create up to four torch-sized lights (or one vaguely humanoid form) that shed dim light; move them as a bonus action.
