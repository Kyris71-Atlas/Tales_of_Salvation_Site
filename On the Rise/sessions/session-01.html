Session 01 · The Fall of Elturel

November 4, 2026 · On the Rise · Power of Three · Elturel → cult site → the road west

Single voice. Session Chronicle Record — table chronicle for the public shelf.

Chronicle · Session 01

From Session Chronicle Record · 11/4/26 · table-safe

Three travelers become the Power of Three beneath the Companion. Cult work outside Elturel. Then the chains rise — and the holy city is gone.

Session frame

  • Party: The Power of Three (Damien, Herman, Tokaninaka) with Reya Mantlemorn
  • Start: A Pair of Black Antlers, Elturel
  • End: Road from the ruins of Elturel toward Baldur’s Gate
  • Objective: Investigate cult activity outside Elturel

Session summary

Beneath the unwavering light of the Companion, three travelers were brought together in the holy city of Elturel.

Damien Lightshadow and Herman the Recluse were already present when Tokana arrived in a far less ordinary fashion. Her journey from the Outer Planes carried her down toward the mortal world and over Elturel itself.

From above, Nina caught a view of the Companion unlike anything visible from the streets below. It was not merely a brilliant light. She could make out a clear, hardened sphere — and within it, something or someone radiant. She could tell no more than that.

Shortly afterward, Reya Mantlemorn gathered the three adventurers together and brought them into the growing investigation surrounding cult activity near Elturel. Before long, Tokaninaka had already given the unlikely trio a name: The Power of Three.

Beneath the Companion

Elturel appeared exactly as a holy city should. The Companion shone overhead. Its light bathed the streets, temples, homes, and people below. There was a sense of safety here — not merely the absence of danger, but the belief that this was a place protected by something greater.

The party took an interest in the Companion and in the feeling it cast across the city. For Nina, the question had begun before she had ever set foot inside Elturel. She had seen enough during her descent to know that there was more to the Companion than anyone standing beneath it could easily perceive.

The wedding

On the road outside the city, the party encountered a wedding beneath a magnificent willow tree. An elderly priest of Lathander presided as a young couple exchanged vows with Elturel standing beautifully in the distance.

There was joking among the travelers that young marriages did not always last. Looking toward the holy city beneath the Companion, another possibility seemed equally reasonable. Perhaps here, of all places, they really did have a good chance. The Power of Three continued on.

The trail

The investigation carried Reya and the party into the woods, where signs of cult activity led them toward an old druidic site. What they found was not a single unified enemy. Followers of several dark powers had gathered in the same area, and evidence of violence between them was already apparent. The party pressed deeper into the site.

The cultists

  • Tokaninaka loosed an arrow at Kest Varian, a Night Blade of Bhaal — one shot was enough.
  • The party captured Drek Zin, a Fist of Bane, and left him bound in the Druid Circle.
  • At the altar: Nereza Sile (Night Blade of Bhaal) and Dorn Halvek (Dragonwing), already a victim of the cult struggle.
  • Nereza drove her blade into Dorn; the Power of Three made short work of her.
  • Dorn lived long enough to speak, then entrusted them with a letter before dying.
  • An unseen Night Blade circled the woods. When the party returned, Drek Zin was gone — two enemies escaped into the countryside.

What they carried away

  • Herman — a Bone of Animation (Damien’s Divine Sense also answered to it).
  • A Rose of Bhaal.
  • Reya — five small dragon statues: red, white, black, green, blue.
  • Dorn Halvek’s letter — importance not yet fully understood.

The return that never arrived

With the cult site behind them, Reya and the party turned back toward Elturel. They never reached it.

Massive infernal chains erupted from the earth. They rose around Elturel and reached toward the Companion above. For a moment it appeared the chains sought the brilliant sphere itself. They could not take hold. Instead the enormous hooks caught the edges of the city — and pulled.

Elturel began to descend. Buildings, streets, temples — the entire holy city — were dragged downward as the chains hauled it into the earth. The Power of Three and Reya could do nothing but watch. Then Elturel was gone. The Companion was gone with it. Where a city had stood only moments before, there remained an enormous crater of torn earth and stone.

The wedding. The willow. The streets beneath the Companion. Everything they had passed through that day had suddenly become part of a place that no longer existed.

Where the road leads

Reya and the Power of Three mounted their horses. Around them the roads filled with others who survived only because they happened to be outside the city when it vanished — travelers, farmers, pilgrims, families, refugees.

Elturel was gone. There was no city to return to. As the survivors gathered on the road, one destination became unavoidable: Baldur’s Gate.

For Reya Mantlemorn and the newly formed Power of Three, the road west had become the only road left.

Chronicle close

They had begun the day beneath the light of a holy city. They ended it watching that city dragged into Hell. And somewhere beyond the road ahead, the truth of what happened to Elturel was waiting.
