Voice 2 · Player recap

Player-provided · Thrain's notebook 7/20/26

We begin the adventure with a short rest. We continue to map the area. One room is full of undead. Benn turns undead. Mantis Thunderwaves. The rug gets involved. The undead are dead. Lard leads the way.

We map to the south, entering a kitchen area. An animated table has become the party’s target. Tallowick slides and shoots with precision. Before it could react, Benn blasted it into pieces. Only a storage area of rotting food.

To the north, a shrine to the gods room, has been defaced. More undead occupy the area. We attack. Everyone fought well and ended a Wight, Skeletal Mage, and 2 Large Ogre Skeletons.

The party TPs to town. Time to rest, heal, and stock up. The black dragon will be our next victim.

Individual stories while in town are welcome (dwarven thumbs up)
