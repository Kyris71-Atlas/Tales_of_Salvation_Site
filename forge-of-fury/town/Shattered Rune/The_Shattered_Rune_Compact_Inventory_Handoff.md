# The Shattered Rune — Compact Inventory Handoff

## Purpose

The Shattered Rune page has limited room, so the shop inventory should be organized into **three compact sections**.

The goal is to give level 6 players meaningful buying options without overcrowding the page.

---

## 1. Everyday Arcana

Keep this section practical, useful, and easy to scan.

- Potion of Healing — 50 gp
- Potion of Greater Healing — 200 gp
- Spell Scrolls:
  - Feather Fall
  - Identify
  - Shield
  - Misty Step
  - Invisibility
  - Web
  - Dispel Magic
  - Fly
- Arcane focus — 10–50 gp
- Component pouch — 25 gp
- Ritual chalks — 25 gp
- Scribing inks — 10 gp per vial
- Fine parchment — 5 gp per sheet

### Display Recommendation

This section should use a compact list rather than full item cards.

---

## 2. Adventurer’s Shelf

These are the items most likely to make players pause and consider a purchase.

- Bag of Holding — 400 gp
- Driftglobe — 300 gp
- Wand of Secrets — 300 gp
- Wand of Magic Detection — 350 gp
- Immovable Rod — 500 gp
- Goggles of Night — 600 gp
- Boots of Elvenkind — 700 gp
- Cloak of Protection — 900 gp
- Pearl of Power — 800 gp
- Sending Stones — 750 gp

### Display Recommendation

Use compact cards or rows with:

- item name;
- price;
- one-line purpose.

Example:

> **Immovable Rod** — 500 gp  
> Press the button and it stays exactly where it is.

---

## 3. Behind the Counter

This section should feel rare, limited, and slightly aspirational.

- Wand of Web — 3,000 gp
- Figurine of Wondrous Power: Silver Raven — 3,500 gp
- Rotating Curiosity — price varies

### Rotating Curiosity Pool

Only one of these should appear at a time:

- Ring of Mind Shielding
- Boots of Levitation
- Stone of Good Luck
- Weapon of Warning
- Broom of Flying
- Bag of Beans

---

## Design Guidance

Keep only these **10 permanent uncommon items** visible on the page:

- Bag of Holding
- Driftglobe
- Wand of Secrets
- Wand of Magic Detection
- Immovable Rod
- Goggles of Night
- Boots of Elvenkind
- Cloak of Protection
- Pearl of Power
- Sending Stones

The larger item pool should remain off-page and be used as rotating stock.

This keeps the shop page readable while allowing Gimble’s inventory to evolve between sessions.
