# Mantis Fishing Easter Egg — Grok Build Handoff


## Project Purpose

Add a hidden easter egg to the Blasingdell Town Hub map near the river where Mantis was fishing at the very beginning of the campaign.

The easter egg should be an **invisible HTML door** placed near the water.

A player who discovers and clicks it should reach a small standalone page that honors the real origin of one of the campaign’s most important threads.

This page is not a lore explanation.

It is a quiet memorial to a player choice made in **Session 1, Minute 1**.

---

## The True Origin

Before Kevin said:

> “Mantis is fishing.”

there was:

- no Deep Memory;
- no Aboleth thread;
- no water mystery;
- no hidden system involving the river;
- no plan for the water to matter.

Kevin’s choice put Mantis beside the water.

Christopher improvised one response:

> **The water is wrong.**

That was all that existed at the time.

Everything else came later.

The correct development order is:

1. Kevin decides Mantis is fishing.
2. That puts Mantis at the river.
3. Christopher improvises that the water feels wrong.
4. The campaign later grows around that unexplained beat.

The lore did not lead Mantis to the river.

**Mantis going to the river created the lore.**

---

## Critical Player-Knowledge Boundary

The easter egg page must not reveal:

- the Aboleth;
- the Deep Memory;
- the Black Lake;
- the Foundry connection;
- Twenty-Six Breaths;
- any hidden cosmology;
- any later explanation for why the water mattered;
- any statement that this moment created or foreshadowed those things;
- any DM-only interpretation of the scene.

The page may honor the moment.

It must not explain the moment.

---

## Town Hub Door

Place one invisible clickable hotspot near the river location where Mantis was fishing in the opening scene. (see town/fishing_location.jpg for point of reference)

The hotspot should be:

- invisible during normal use;
- a real accessible link;
- keyboard-focusable;
- discoverable through deliberate exploration;
- visually non-disruptive.

The Sir Kluth “Terms and Conditions” hidden-link treatment is a useful reference for the spirit of the interaction, but this should remain its own implementation.

Do not make it easy enough that it stops being an easter egg.

---

## Easter Egg Page Tone

The page should feel:

- quiet;
- reflective;
- grateful;
- curious;
- small;
- personal;
- mysterious.

It should not feel:

- ominous;
- explanatory;
- encyclopedic;
- like a lore dump;
- like a Deep Memory page;
- like a DM confession.

---

## Suggested Page Structure

### Minimal Header

Page title:

**The First Cast**

### The Question

> What were you doing in Blasingdell before the Company met?

### The Answer

> Kevin said Mantis was fishing.

### The Moment

> His line was deep in the water.  
> Something felt wrong.

Do not add an explanation.

### The Questions

Use a small group of unanswered reflective questions:

- What makes a passing choice worth remembering?
- When does a character begin shaping the world around them?
- How much can grow from one answer given before anyone knows it matters?
- What did Mantis feel beneath the water?
- Was anything there yet?

Optional final line:

> **Mantis chose the water first.**

### Return Link

Include a quiet link back to the Blasingdell Town Hub.

Do not add this page to standard site navigation.

---

## Visual Direction

Keep the page visually simple.

Possible elements:

- dark or muted water texture;
- a faint fishing line descending into deep water;
- a small ripple;
- subdued blue-gray tones;
- one soft pulse beneath the surface;
- restrained typography;
- generous empty space.

Avoid:

- visible monsters;
- tentacles;
- eyes;
- psychic imagery;
- Deep Memory motifs;
- black obelisks;
- underwater ruins;
- overt horror;
- anything that answers the mystery.

The page should suggest uncertainty, not danger with a known shape.

## Implementation Guidance

- Build one small standalone HTML page.
- Keep it outside normal navigation.
- Link it only through the hidden river hotspot.
- Maintain existing site CSS where practical.
- Use minimal custom CSS.
- Preserve accessibility.
- Avoid JavaScript unless needed for a subtle effect.
- Keep animation lightweight.
- Do not create hidden DM notes in comments.

---

## Design Goal

**A player who discovers this page should feel that they found a quiet thank-you to a meaningful opening moment, not an explanation of what the moment later became.**

## Non-Negotiable Truth

Before Kevin said Mantis was fishing, there was no plan for the water.

That player choice created the opening.

The easter egg exists to honor that.
