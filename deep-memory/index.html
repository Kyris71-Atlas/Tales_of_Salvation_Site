Deep Memory

Far Realm vault · every campaign is worth keeping

What this vault is

Deep Memory is not a submenu of one campaign. It is the Far Realm entity and site vault that holds what every table lived: session recaps, Character Discovery Reports (dossiers), companions, and the threads that connect them.

Each campaign is a chamber inside the vault. You enter memory here, then open a chamber door — you do not have to pass through Campaign Home first (though active play still keeps a shortcut from Ravenloft’s Fury).

This is not a play sheet. For Tempest Company’s Company Character Sheets, start on Ravenloft’s Fury Campaign Home (Forge of Fury is the archived chapter).

Session recaps live inside chambers. Most chambers use one voice (table notes from in-game MD and/or recordings). Forge of Fury is the exception: two voices when a player also supplies a written recap.

Three Strands of Memory

Company Character Sheet

Table tool for Tempest Company PCs — fast stats for play. Active campaign only. Not used for archived campaigns or NPC companions.

Discovery Report

Atlas archive page — evidence, story, and meaning as they are earned. Lives under Deep Memory. Grows over time; incomplete is honest.

Companions

Always NPCs. Some use a Fantasy Grounds character sheet for convenience — they remain companions, not company player characters. No CCS.
