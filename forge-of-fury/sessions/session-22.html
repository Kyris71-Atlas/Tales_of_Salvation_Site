Session 22

10/5/26 · Ravenloft’s Fury · Tempest Company · Gulthias falls · Wizard of Wines approach

Tempest Company — two voices. Voice 1 — table notes (session record). Voice 2 — player-provided recap.

Voice 1 · Table notes

From session record · 10/5/26 · table-safe

Yester Hill finishes. The tree dies. The Tome finds a bearer — then the vineyard starts to move.

Root network & tree

  • Friendly raven signals: fighting the tree is the right call.
  • Lard plants the Gulthias Staff into the roots (Sanity 16, Athletics 22); Mantis Lightning Bolt through the staff (24 lightning into the network).
  • Party finishes the Gulthias Tree — Fire Bolt, Dragon Slayer, steel. Tree explodes; weather eases; senses return.

The Tome

  • Tome of Strahd revealed under the ruined roots. Lard claims it — and keeps the staff.
  • Lard fails a Sanity check afterward: the story is repeating, but this telling cannot be trusted.
  • Bags of Holding do not lift the Tome out of Barovia in this telling.

Leaving the hill

  • Mira released. Berserker ally stays with the Company.
  • Travel to Wizard of Wines; Ireena bitten by Strahd; plan toward a hunter’s tower for her safety.

Cliffhanger

  • Night falls. Nerys and Torik shift hybrid. Hostiles close through the vineyard rows.
  • Session ends with battle about to begin outside the winery.

Notes expand when more is thrown at Atlas. Same session as the player recap — different job.

Voice 2 · Player recap

Player-provided · Thrain’s notebook 10/5/26

The raven, Thrain believes is friendly, shows the party that fighting the tree is what it wants. Lard and Mantis devise a plan to shock the plants through the root system. Electricity pops all but 2 plant wrath’s heads. The Gulthias Tree stinks as it takes damage. We destroy the tree and the weather immediately changes. Clouds thin but no sun shines just yet. The rain stops and our senses that, unbeknownst to us were dulled, has begun returning to normal. Benn and the raven communicate. Lard retrieves the book that called to him. Lards face drops as he recognizes the symbol on its cover.

We travel to the winery where we’re introduced. Ireena has been bitten by a vampire, Strahad. He is trying to get at her again. Our goal is to get her to a tower where a famous legendary hunter should be able to keep her safe. The world starts to turn to night. Creatures emerge from the woods. 2 magic users and 2 werwolves eye us up.
