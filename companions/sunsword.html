Sunsword

Sentient sun blade · Sergei’s sword · Van Richten’s Tower
Legacy weapon Ravenloft’s Fury Card 3 — The Wizard Rare · Attunement
Reading Wizard — Sunsword → Van Richten’s Tower Form Longsword hilt · blade of radiance · finesse FG 001 Ravenloft’s Fury (campaigns) Atrok
What it is

To the eye it is only a longsword hilt. Grasp it, and a bonus action calls a blade of pure radiance into being — or banishes it again. While the blade exists, this is a magic longsword with the finesse property. Proficiency with shortswords or longswords covers the Sunsword.

It was once Sergei von Zarovich’s crystal blade. The hilt survived. The crystal did not. What remains has taken on the nature of a sun blade.

How the Reading Points Here

The Wizard card named this weapon and placed it at Van Richten’s Tower. Tempest Company has reached the place. The hunter has kept the blade close — and used it sparingly — because sunlight in Barovia is both shield and beacon. The sword wants Strahd destroyed. Revenge for a broken crystal matters more to it than freeing the land.

In the hand

+2 to attack and damage. Deals radiant damage instead of slashing. Against undead, an extra 1d8 radiant. Versatile (1d10) when the blade is present.

Sunlight

The luminous blade sheds bright light 15 ft. and dim light 15 ft. more. That light is sunlight. An action expands or reduces each radius by 5 feet (min 10 / max 30).

Sentience

Chaotic good. Int 11 · Wis 17 · Cha 16. Hearing and normal vision 60 ft. Speaks in emotions to whoever carries or wields it. It fears its own destruction even as it hungers for Strahd’s.

Lore from the FG record

Strahd ordered the wizard Khazan to destroy Sergei’s sword. Khazan separated hilt from crystal blade; an apprentice stole the hilt and died in the Svalich Woods. Khazan told Strahd the whole weapon was gone. The hilt knows the crystal is lost forever — and still burns for what comes next.
