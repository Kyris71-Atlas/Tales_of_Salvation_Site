On the Rise portrayal
This table’s Reya is not the Low Lantern fugitive intro from the printed adventure. She met the party in Elturel, led them into the cult work beyond the walls, and fled west after the Fall. Public page keeps to what the Session Chronicle already holds.

Combat

Longsword +1. Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1d8 + 4 slashing (versatile 1d10 + 4). Magic weapon.

Shortsword. Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1d6 + 3 piercing. Finesse · light.

Heavy Crossbow. Ranged Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, range 100/400 ft., one target. Hit: 1d10 + 1 piercing. Heavy · loading · two-handed.

Improved Critical. Weapon attacks crit on 19–20.

Class features & feats

Second Wind. Bonus action: regain 1d10 + 5 hit points. Once per short or long rest.

Action Surge. Take one additional action on your turn. Once per short or long rest.

Military Rank. Hellriders and allied soldiers of lower rank recognize her authority; she can often requisition simple gear or horses for temporary use.

Heavy Armor Master. While wearing heavy armor, reduce nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage by 3.

Mobile. Speed +10 ft. Dash ignores difficult terrain that turn. After a melee attack against a creature, no opportunity attacks from that creature for the rest of the turn.

  • Tied to Elturel’s fate and the road that began when the city fell.
