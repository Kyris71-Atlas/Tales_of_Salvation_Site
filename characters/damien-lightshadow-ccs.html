Divine Sense
Uses: / 4 · Action · long rest

Until the end of your next turn, know the location of any celestial, fiend, or undead within 60 feet (not behind total cover), and any consecrated or desecrated place or object in that radius. You know the type, not the identity.

Lay on Hands
Pool: / 5 · Action · long rest

Touch a creature; restore hit points from the pool (up to the amount remaining). Alternatively, spend 5 from the pool to cure one disease or neutralize one poison. No effect on undead or constructs.

Thaumaturgy
Cantrip · Legacy of Avernus · CHA · range 30 ft

Manifest a minor wonder (booming voice, flickering flames, tremors, sound, slam a door, alter your eyes). Up to three 1-minute effects at once; dismiss one as an action.

Background · Knight of the Order

Shelter, meals, and healing from members of your order and those sympathetic to its aims — occasionally riskier aid when you are hard-pressed or hunted unjustly.
