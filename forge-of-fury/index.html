Session 18 · 8/24/26 · Thrain's Session Recap

Gimble bids us farewell. We climb down the chain ladder to the bottom of the waterfall. The walls are slick and there’s not a lot of room to move around. To the east, starting to crumble bridges, allow the party to continue deeper into the cave. There it is!! Lard points out where he sees the dragon. The ranged attackers begin to shoot their shots. Qiran casts flying onto Lard. Electric shocks shoot from Mantis’s and Qiran’s attacks. Benn does something. Lards swooping attacks hit hard. Qiran taunts Nightscale by kicking some of its horde into the water. That angered the dragon and it quickly attacks but Qiran was sly and escaped its grasp. With a great leap of faith into the water, Thrain hurls a hand axe that causes the final blow.

Loot is collected and we return to town. According to the contract, we had to turn over our findings. The head of Nightscale is dropped at The Lady’s feet, proving its demise.

At the Pick & Lantern, a flamboyant looking woman busts into the joint. All eyes seem to attract to her as she obviously looks out of place. Immediately sitting at The Tempest table, she sternly requests for the black card we found. A very cunning move by Lard, he never divulged that find and kept it to himself. After a short conversation we hand over the card and Zerlinka begins to read our fortune. It made no sense to us. We offer our services to find her other missing cards but only gave a vague response. She took the card and left. What was that? Wondering what that card meant. Wonder what that reading meant! What’s next for our adventurers? Stay tuned next week.
