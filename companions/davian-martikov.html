From the FG record
Personality: I judge people by what they do when no one is watching. I trust slowly, but once someone proves themselves, I protect them fiercely.
Ideal: Resistance. Evil survives when good people stay isolated. We endure by protecting one another and sharing what we know.
Bond: My family and the Keepers of the Feather are all parts of the same duty: preserving hope in Barovia.
Flaw: I would rather risk my own life than ask another member of the flock to take a danger I believe I can bear.

Traits

Shapechanger. As an action, polymorphs into a raven-humanoid hybrid or a Tiny raven, or back into humanoid form. Statistics other than size and speed stay the same. Equipment is not transformed. Reverts on death.

Regeneration. Regains 10 hit points at the start of its turn. Silvered weapons or spell damage prevent regeneration until the start of the next turn. Dies only if it starts a turn at 0 HP and does not regenerate.

Mimicry. Can mimic simple sounds heard before. A creature that hears them can tell they are imitations with a DC 10 Wisdom (Insight) check.

Mobile. In humanoid and hybrid form, walking and fly speed increase by 10 feet. When using Dash, difficult terrain costs no extra movement that turn. After a melee attack against a creature, does not provoke opportunity attacks from that creature for the rest of the turn.

Actions & Reactions

Multiattack (Humanoid or Hybrid Form Only). Two weapon attacks, one of which can be with its hand crossbow.

Shortsword (Humanoid or Hybrid Form Only). Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.

Hand Crossbow (Humanoid or Hybrid Form Only). Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.

Beak (Raven or Hybrid Form Only). Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1 piercing (raven) or 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing (hybrid). Humanoid targets must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution save or be cursed with wereraven lycanthropy.

Protect the Flock (Reaction). When a creature Davian can see within 5 feet is hit by an attack, he can reduce the damage by 1d8 + 3.
