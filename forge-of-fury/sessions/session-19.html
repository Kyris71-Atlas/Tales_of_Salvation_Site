Voice 2 · Player recap

Player-provided · Thrain’s notebook 8/31/26

Our reward reaps riches. There’s a plethora of loot to sell and a lot to buy. Trades, merchandising, haggling and sweet dealings increase the party’s power as they prepare to continue their quest.

Weeks go by and things start to get a little closer to normal. One late night at the pick and lantern, a heavy fog rolls in. Lard realizes it’s a fog that reminds him of Barovia. It envelops the party and crackles all around. A campfire starts to become visible, yet everything around, as the fog slowly reveals the new surroundings. Lard shares his sad story of the fog taking his father. A voice is heard. Qiran introduces himself and Mantis offers fish but the man continues to be a babbling Druid. Dripping. Dripping black. Dripping black ooze is all over the Druid and the tree he’s been babbling to. Heading north, we leave the fire. “Safety is an illusion in these lands”. We stumble upon an encampment where a group wants to use Winter Splinter to take down a winery. None of this makes any sense to the party but we prefer to side with these characters, for the time being, than the Druid.
