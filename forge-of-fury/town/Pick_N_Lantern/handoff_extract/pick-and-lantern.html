The Pick & Lantern

Rufflin Coalhand Marta Brindle Brennar Tallwick Bilt Flintcap Orrin Barleycross Edda Bellweather Toman Vell Pella Nack Hark Durn Sella Marr

Warm lantern light spills across the common room as mugs strike tables and a dwarven chorus misses the same note together.

Tempest Company has heard these stories before. That does not mean the town has heard them correctly.

How stories travel in Blasingdell: Mapfoot tells what he saw. Lady Vellbrandt places useful truths and careful half-truths with the right people. Tempest Company returns through the portal carrying scars, strange loot, and incomplete explanations. By morning, the Pick & Lantern has done the rest.

What Blasingdell Says

Rufflin Coalhand says:Retired shift boss · Northwest wall

“Mantis does the talking, sure. He’s the Voice of Tempest Company.”

“But watch Bearblood before the little storm-mage answers. Thrain’s already checked the doors, counted the trouble, and decided which road gets everyone home.”

“He leads from the rear. Smart place to stand when everyone in front keeps finding monsters.”

Thrain · Mostly true

Marta “Two-Tankard” Brindle says:Proprietor · Knows which version started where

“Tempest Company comes through that door carrying less food, more scars, and something nobody in town has seen before.”

“They eat, they drink, they answer three questions out of twenty, and by sunrise everyone in Blasingdell knows seven different versions of what happened.”

“Mine’s usually the closest.”

The Company · Reliable source

Brennar “Mapfoot” Tallwick says:Cartographer · Eyewitness · Crossbow enthusiast

“Qiran and Thrain listened when I described what I saw. Properly listened. You remember that when you are underground and everyone else is deciding whether the cartographer counts as an adventurer.”

“And the crossbow? Well, yes. I have finished off a few creatures.”

“It happens all the time.”

Mapfoot · Eyewitness

Bilt Flintcap says:Greytooth miner · Survived the Cracked Vein

“Twenty-six of us went down at once. Not staggered. Not one after another. One breath we were working, and the next—nothing.”

“Some woke where they fell. Some dreamed. Some still disagree about what they remember.”

“Tempest Company went below while everyone else was deciding how far away to stand.”

The Cracked Vein · Firsthand

Orrin Barleycross says:Produce hauler · Farmer Benn enthusiast

“Farmer Benn looks harmless until somebody needs help.”

“Then he walks toward the thing everyone else is backing away from.”

“Five adventurers keep entering that mountain, and five keep coming back. That little farmer must be the finest healer north of the river.”

Benn · Brave truth, faulty arithmetic

Edda Bellweather says:Shrine regular · Evening bell volunteer

“Sister Maerith includes Tempest Company in her daily blessings.”

“Every morning.”

“Health for the wounded. Courage for those below. Wisdom enough to know when to return.”

“She says the last part a little louder.”

The Shrine · Well known

Toman Vell says:Caravan hand · Has heard Gimble tell this many times

“Gimble Wandsplice had a shipment disappear on the road—rare chalks, strange inks, gemstones ground down for some magical business.”

“Tempest Company brought it back.”

“The gnome has been grateful ever since. Loudly grateful. Frequently grateful. Usually while trying to sell them something.”

The Shattered Rune · Confirmed

Pella Nack says:Alehouse bookkeeper · Notices who checks their pockets

“Mantis put Rilkin Shadecloak to sleep. Just dropped him.”

“When Rilkin woke, he checked every pocket, started making a scene, then looked around and decided perhaps he had misplaced nothing after all.”

“Nobody knows what went missing.”

“That has not stopped anyone from naming it.”

Mantis & Rilkin · Uncertain ending

Hark Durn says:Tunnel-brace carpenter · Lard scholar

“Lard once held up a collapsing tunnel while six miners escaped.”

“Someone says it was twelve.”

“Someone else says the tunnel apologized.”

“I believe the last one.”

Lard · Increasingly impossible

Sella Marr says:Traveling cloth merchant · Collects stories with her orders

“Qiran came back carrying a blade that belonged to a dead knight.”

“Or the knight came back carrying Qiran. Depends who tells it.”

“Either way, there are moments when he looks toward empty space like someone has just offered him advice.”

Qiran · Nobody agrees

These are player-facing recaps, tavern stories, and public reputations—not a complete or authoritative account of what happened beneath Stone Tooth.
