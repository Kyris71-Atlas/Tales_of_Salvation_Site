## Two-Image Banner Sequence

The easter egg page should use two wide banner images as a connected visual sequence.

### Image 1 — `above.png`

This is the opening view.

It should show:

- Mantis fishing beside the river;
- The Shrine visible behind him;
- a calm, ordinary moment in Blasingdell;
- the fishing line cast into the water;
- no visible threat;
- no obvious supernatural effect.

This image establishes the player choice:

> Mantis is fishing.

The tone should be peaceful, grounded, and almost mundane.

---

### Image 2 — `below.png`

This image should appear to continue directly beneath the first.

It should show:

- the same fishing line descending naturally into the water;
- the hook suspended below the surface;
- the worm no longer intact on the hook;
- small pieces of the worm drifting around it;
- no gore;
- no visible creature;
- no identifiable source of the damage.

Only the water within an approximate **10-foot-diameter area around the hook** should behave irregularly.

The localized effect should feel:

- subtle;
- psychic;
- quiet;
- unnatural;
- centered on the hook;
- limited in scope.

Outside that localized area, the water should remain calm and normal.

Avoid:

- a large vortex;
- a broad whirlpool;
- dramatic suction;
- movement affecting the full river;
- anything traveling visibly through the entire water column;
- anything that reveals a creature or known cause.

The visual implication is that a subtle force has killed the worm and disturbed only the nearby water.

---

## Combined Visual Reading

The two images should read as one continuous scene.

**Above:** peaceful Blasingdell, Mantis fishing beside The Shrine.

**Below:** the line reaches a small area where the water behaves incorrectly.

The contrast is essential.

Nothing in the first image should warn the viewer.

Nothing in the second image should explain what happened.

Together, the sequence should leave the audience with one clear impression:

> **The water is wrong.**

Do not add explanatory captions, DM lore, or later campaign context between the images.
