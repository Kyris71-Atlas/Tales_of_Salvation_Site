Durnan Ironvein is a squat, formidable dwarf blacksmith running a stone-and-timber workshop with a tall chimney, constant smoke, and the sound of hammer on steel carrying down the street.

The source gives us an important distinction:

Most of the forge is dedicated to mining gear: pickaxes, chisels, reinforced wheelbarrows.
Durnan is capable of producing weapons and shields on commission.

That means the page should not feel like a generic fantasy weapons warehouse. It should feel like a working industrial smithy that also serves adventurers.

Page direction
Arrival

A strong sensory opening:

Heat rolls through the doorway before you cross the threshold. Hammer blows ring from the rear forge, sparks jump against black stone, and Durnan Ironvein looks up only after finishing the strike already in motion.

Distinctive shop voice

Gimble sells possibilities.

Durnan sells things that will still work after something tries to kill you.

A good shop line might be:

Steel for the mine. Iron for the road. Better work for those who know the difference.

And Durnan’s tone should stay practical:

“Pretty costs extra. Strong doesn’t.”

The next visual step should mirror what we did for Gimble: a wide smithy banner with Durnan near the counter or anvil, finished weapons and shields displayed behind him, while the working forge remains visible in the background.
