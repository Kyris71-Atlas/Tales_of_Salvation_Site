The Shrine

Welcome

Sunlight catches on glass bottles and fresh-cut herbs as Sister Maerith works behind a table set outside the shrine. Healing draughts, miners’ tonics, bandages, and poultices surround her, while candles burn quietly beside the modest altar within.

Lady Seraphine Vellbrandt has encouraged and funded Maerith’s potion-making so the Shrine can better serve the miners’ Wellbeing — more Remedies, fewer hard days left untreated. Maerith believes every bottle that goes out is one less miner who has to grin through it.

Potions for the Wounded. Remedies for the Road. Blessings for those going Below.

Sister Maerith

Young, sincere, and good at her Work — devoted to Blasingdell’s miners and townsfolk. She wants better tools, better remedies, and fewer people suffering. Her goodness is real. Her handwriting on the boards is… enthusiastic.
