# Ironvein Smithy — Compact Shop List Handoff

## Shop Identity

Ironvein Smithy is a working stone-and-timber forge run by **Durnan Ironvein**.

Most of Durnan’s daily work supports Blasingdell’s mines: pickaxes, chisels, reinforced fittings, and wheelbarrow hardware. He also produces dependable weapons, shields, and armor work for adventurers.

The page should feel like a practical smithy, not a fully stocked fantasy weapons warehouse.

> **Steel for the mine. Iron for the road. Better work for those who know the difference.**

## 2. Repairs and Commission Work

This is the section that gives Durnan’s shop its character.

- Weapon cleaning and sharpening — 1–5 gp
- Minor weapon repair — 5 gp
- Major weapon repair — price after inspection
- Shield repair — 5–15 gp
- Armor patching — 10–50 gp
- Armor resizing or refitting — 25–100 gp
- Replace straps, buckles, or fittings — 2–10 gp
- Silver one weapon or 10 pieces of ammunition — 100 gp
- Custom shield commission — starting at 25 gp
- Custom weapon commission — base weapon cost plus labor
- Decorative engraving — starting at 10 gp
- Durgeddin-style mark or dwarven detailing — on commission 

### Commission Note

Custom work should require:

- a deposit;
- an agreed design;
- completion date available on request.

Durnan prioritizes strength, balance, and function over decoration.

> “Pretty costs extra. Strong doesn’t.”

## 3. Durnan Will Buy

Durnan is interested in useful metalwork, salvage, and equipment he can restore or resell.

### Usually Accepted

- martial and simple weapons;
- shields;
- medium and heavy armor;
- metal ammunition;
- mining tools;
- usable metal salvage;
- ingots and workable ore;
- damaged gear with recoverable parts.

### Requires Appraisal

- dwarven weapons or armor;
- unusual alloys;
- Durgeddin-marked pieces;
- magical weapons, armor, or shields;
- badly damaged relics;
- items of uncertain origin.

### Usually Refused

- cloth or leather goods;
- ordinary jewelry;
- arcane curios;
- badly corroded scrap with no workable metal;
- stolen goods that are obviously identifiable.

### Selling Guidance

Final purchase values remain a DM decision.

As a general presentation rule, the page may state:

> Durnan pays according to condition, usefulness, and how much work it will take to put an item back on the rack.

## Optional Featured Work

Show only one or two special pieces at a time.

Possible rotating features:

- Masterwork Shield
- Silvered Longsword
- Reinforced Breastplate
- Dwarven Warhammer
- Commission Slot Available
- Unusual Metal Awaiting a Buyer

These should feel like current workshop projects, not permanent stock.

---

## Design Guidance

Keep the page to three visible commerce sections:

1. **Ready from the Rack**
2. **Repairs and Commission Work**
3. **Durnan Will Buy**

The banner and arrival text should establish the heat, hammering, sparks, and working-forge atmosphere.

The shop list should remain practical, readable, and easy for Christopher to update between sessions.
