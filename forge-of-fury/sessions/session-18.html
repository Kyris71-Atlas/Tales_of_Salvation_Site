Session 18

8/24/26 · Forge of Fury · Tempest Company · Nightscale falls · Pick & Lantern

FoF dual-voice exception. Voice 1 — table notes (session record). Voice 2 — player-provided recap.

Voice 1 · Table notes

From session record · 8/24/26 · table-safe

Nightscale defeated. Contract proof delivered. A stranger claims a dry card from the hoard.

Approach

  • Gimble farewells the company.
  • Chain ladder down the waterfall; slick walls; little footing.
  • East along crumbling bridges deeper into the cave.
  • Lard spots Nightscale.

The fight

  • Ranged open fire; Qiran casts Fly on Lard.
  • Lightning from Mantis and Qiran; Lard’s swooping attacks land hard.
  • Qiran kicks hoard treasure into the water — taunt; Nightscale grapples, Qiran escapes.
  • Thrain’s leap and hand axe deliver the finishing blow.

Aftermath & town

  • Loot gathered; Nightscale’s head taken as proof.
  • Return to Blasingdell; contract findings turned over; head dropped at Lady Vellbrandt’s feet.
  • Among the hoard: a single Tarokka card, perfectly dry.

Pick & Lantern

  • Flamboyant stranger enters; sits at Tempest’s table; demands the black card.
  • Lard had not advertised the find; after talk, the card is handed over.
  • She reads a fortune the company does not understand; vague on help finding other missing cards; takes the card and leaves.
  • (Player notebook names her Zerlinka; table planning notes also use Zulika — same encounter.)

State

  • Nightscale is dead. Forge of Fury’s dragon beat is closed for now.
  • Company is in town at the Pick & Lantern. What’s next remains open.

Notes expand when more is thrown at Atlas. Same session as the player recap — different job.

Voice 2 · Player recap

Player-provided · Thrain's notebook 8/24/26

Gimble bids us farewell. We climb down the chain ladder to the bottom of the waterfall. The walls are slick and there’s not a lot of room to move around. To the east, starting to crumble bridges, allow the party to continue deeper into the cave. There it is!! Lard points out where he sees the dragon. The ranged attackers begin to shoot their shots. Qiran casts flying onto Lard. Electric shocks shoot from Mantis’s and Qiran’s attacks. Benn does something. Lards swooping attacks hit hard. Qiran taunts Nightscale by kicking some of its horde into the water. That angered the dragon and it quickly attacks but Qiran was sly and escaped its grasp. With a great leap of faith into the water, Thrain hurls a hand axe that causes the final blow.

Loot is collected and we return to town. According to the contract, we had to turn over our findings. The head of Nightscale is dropped at The Lady’s feet, proving its demise.

At the pick & lantern, a flamboyant looking woman busts into the joint. All eyes seem to attract to her as she obviously looks out of place. Immediately sitting at The Tempest table, she sternly requests for the black card we found. A very cunning move by Lard, he never divulged that find and kept it to himself. After a short conversation we hand over the card and Zerlinka begins to read our fortune. It made no sense to us. We offer our services to find her other missing cards but only gave a vague response. She took the card and left. What was that? Wondering what that card meant. Wonder what that reading meant! What’s next for our adventurers? Stay tuned next week.
