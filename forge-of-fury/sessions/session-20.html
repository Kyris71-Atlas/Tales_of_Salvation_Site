Session 20

9/7/26 · Ravenloft’s Fury · Tempest Company · Yester Hill · Wintersplinter’s threshold

Tempest Company — two voices. Voice 1 — table notes (session record). Voice 2 — player-provided recap.

Voice 1 · Table notes

From session record · 9/7/26 · table-safe

Still on Yester Hill. Talk before steel — then the ritual and the fight tilt hard.

Talk on the hill

  • Company keeps talking with the berserkers; Lard’s prior nat 20 still opens doors (Svala).
  • Lard urges restraint — help if possible; avoid making enemies for free.
  • Benn distrusts the berserkers; nat 20 Insight — Svala’s respect for Lard is real, but the druids are using the berserkers more than the reverse.

Ritual & interference

  • Ritual advances; Mantis grounds roughly half the electrical feed — delay, not a clean stop.
  • A strange bat draws Benn’s attention; Thrain studies it (nat 20) — too deliberate to be ordinary.
  • Benn tests with Sacred Flame (misses); scrutiny on that bat rises.
  • Arosa, a wereraven ally, lands on Benn’s shoulder — Keepers of the Feather presence made visible.

Blood on the stones

  • Fighting spreads; lightning and bodies; Svala joins Tempest’s side when the odds tilt.
  • The babbling Druid had been charmed — Qiran kills him anyway.
  • Session breaks mid-conflict; Wintersplinter’s birth still hangs over the hill.

State

  • Berserkers remain a possible social pivot; Arosa is an open ally.
  • Ritual disrupted but not finished; loyalties on the hill are in play.

Notes expand when more is thrown at Atlas. Same session as the player recap — different job.

Voice 2 · Player recap

Player-provided · Thrain’s notebook 9/7/26

Brannok and Svala step into the pathway that leads to a circular area surrounded by walls of stones. Druids are chanting. It is revealed that they’re attempting to summon someone who will not be good for the wizards or a nearby winery. Lard offers our services for information that will help us understand where we’re at and why. The babbling Druid starts to run up the hill towards us until Qiran scared him off. Mantis stops an electrical charge heading towards the statue that seems to be the focus of the casting druids. The Druids whose actions were halted have taken offense to his disruption. A bat catches Benns eye and becomes extremely attentive to it. He feels extreme evil from it and reaches out for anyone to look too. Thrain noticed the bat, but it’s not a bat. It is a very intelligent creature in disguise. It’s flying faster than what a bat should and it’s heading right at the babbling Druid. Lard attacks to the statue gets everyone’s attention. Shellele!! Mantis is attacked. Quick maneuvering kills the attacker. Svala notices how tilted the fight has become and she starts fighting alongside our party. Bodies start dropping, bats move into formation, booms come from the lightning.

We have to take a break, but we’ll be back next week.
