Session 19 · 8/31/26 · Thrain’s Session Recap · placeholder

Awaiting Thrain’s words. Until the recap lands, here is the shape of Session 19 — Blasingdell business finished, then the Mists, then Yester Hill.

Nightscale’s hoard was divided and upgrades were set in motion: Ironvein work on returning enchantments and +1 gear, bracelets bound for Gimble to become Bracers of Defense. Then the Mists came. Mantis, being Mantis, asked the Mist if it wanted some fish. No clear answer.

Tempest Company arrived on Yester Hill near the Gulthias Tree. Lard somehow talked a berserker into an opening with a natural 20. Lightning struck the standing stones — once, then again — and Mantis saw the pattern: the druids meant to wake the great wooden statue.

The board is set. Something enormous is about to awaken. Thrain’s full Session Recap replaces this placeholder when ready.
