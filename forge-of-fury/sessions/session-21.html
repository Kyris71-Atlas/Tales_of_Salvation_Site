Session 21

9/14/26 · Ravenloft’s Fury · Tempest Company · Yester Hill · Wintersplinter denied

Tempest Company — two voices. Voice 1 — table notes (session record). Voice 2 — player-provided recap.

Voice 1 · Table notes

From session record · 9/14/26 · table-safe

Final stretch on Yester Hill. Ritual on its last round — and a new blade joins the Company.

Talaris arrives

  • Brenda’s new character: Talaris, High Elf Assassin Rogue (level 6). Full in-world intro still open.
  • Round 10 of 10 — statue one initiative from awakening. Talaris nat 20 destroys Wintersplinter’s vessel.
  • Ritual fails. Wintersplinter never takes a step.

The not-a-bat

  • Benn’s Wind Wall pins the strange bat; Mantis Lightning Bolt hits it plus two ordinary bats.
  • Benn Guiding Bolt follows; the creature vanishes. When the wall drops, it is gone.

Staff, tree, and Tome

  • Lard drives the Gulthias Staff into the root network — locks like a live line; Beacon of Hope helps him break free.
  • Brief connection: Gulthias Tree, the land, and the nearby Tome of Strahd. Staff stays with Lard.
  • Tome remains unclaimed at session close.

State

  • Mira Blackbriar surrenders; Lard gags her. She is captive.
  • Main combat mostly done; weather begins to ease; Company noticed on the hill.

Notes expand when more is thrown at Atlas. Same session as the player recap — different job.

Voice 2 · Player recap

Player-provided · Thrain’s notebook 9/14/26

Amidst the melee; weapons clash, bolts are thrown, fish is fried. Another ring around the rocks is stopped by Mantis. The rain falls a bit heavier. Another crash of lightning, that was noticeably out of the ordinary, illuminates a silhouette of another character. Arrows shot from Talaris blasts the statue. More fighting, more fish. The eyes of the statue start to form but Talaris blasts it again. The statue crumbles. A Druid cries out in agony. Benn takes on the bats. The rain starts to get annoying. Most enemies are defeated. We decided to take Mira hostage. We investigate the area and the rain begins to let up. Lard picks up the goopy staff and has a memory of the card reading we received. Having a feeling that there’s something to be found near the necrotic tree, he also believes that retrieving it could become difficult. There are many necrotic beings in the area and waaaay too many plans are talked about. Lard places the staff into ground and suddenly becomes stricken by necrotic damage. We start chopping the tree in the center that feels like it needs to be defeated but can only chip away. Thrain just watches.
