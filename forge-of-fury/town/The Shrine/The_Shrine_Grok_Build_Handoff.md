# The Shrine — Grok Build Handoff

## Project Purpose

Build **The Shrine** page as one of the active locations in the Blasingdell Town Hub.

This page serves two roles at once:

1. A sincere, modest place of healing, solace, and prayer.
2. A small but growing potion-making station funded by Lady Seraphine Vellbrandt to “better serve the miners’ wellbeing.”

The page is player-facing and should feel warm, trustworthy, useful, and grounded in the life of Blasingdell.

---

## Core Character: Sister Maerith

Sister Maerith is:

- young;
- sincere;
- compassionate;
- competent;
- practical;
- devoted to helping Blasingdell’s miners and townsfolk.

She must **not** read as foolish, compromised, secretly corrupt, or knowingly involved in Seraphine’s hidden plans.

Her goodness is real.

The dramatic truth is that Seraphine is exploiting Maerith’s virtue and growing skill, not exposing hypocrisy.

### Character Principle

> Maerith sees better tools, better remedies, and fewer miners suffering.

She genuinely believes her potion-making is improving the lives of the people she serves.

---

## Public Story

Lady Seraphine Vellbrandt, acting as mayor and benefactor, has encouraged and funded Maerith’s potion-making so the Shrine can better support the miners.

Publicly, this means:

- improved access to healing;
- better recovery after accidents;
- remedies for dangerous work underground;
- potions that help miners climb, endure, focus, resist hazards, or recover faster;
- a more capable Shrine serving the town.

This support should appear benevolent and credible on the player-facing page.

---

## DM-Only Truth

Seraphine is quietly creating an **unknowing potion ward** for her own future purposes.

By funding ingredients, equipment, experimentation, and Maerith’s confidence, Seraphine is building:

- a trained potion-maker;
- a respectable production site;
- tested formulas;
- trusted distribution;
- a location no one would think to question.

Maerith does not know this.

Do not reveal or hint at this truth in visible text, tooltips, metadata, comments, navigation, or imagery unless Christopher explicitly approves it.

---

## Visual Direction

Use the newly created panoramic Shrine banner as the hero image.

The banner should establish:

- modest whitewashed stone architecture;
- warm daylight and candlelight;
- Sister Maerith as the focal figure;
- healing supplies;
- herbs;
- potion bottles;
- bandages;
- restorative salves;
- a simple altar and healing nook;
- the sense that the Shrine has grown into a practical potion-making station.

The page should remain visually distinct from:

- The Shattered Rune, which is arcane and eccentric;
- Ironvein Smithy, which is industrial and practical.

The Shrine should feel gentle, clean, pastoral, and quietly useful.

---

## Suggested Arrival Text

> Sunlight catches on glass bottles and fresh-cut herbs as Sister Maerith works behind a table set outside the shrine. Healing draughts, miners’ tonics, bandages, and poultices surround her, while candles burn quietly beside the modest altar within.

> **Potions for the wounded. Remedies for the road. Blessings for those going below.**

---

## Page Structure

Keep the commerce portion to **three compact sections**.

### 1. Healing Potions

This is the Shrine’s core stock.

Recommended items:

- Potion of Healing
- Potion of Greater Healing
- Potion of Superior Healing — limited stock
- Herbal Restorative
- Antitoxin
- Keoghtom’s Ointment — limited or special stock

The exact prices and quantities should remain editable by Christopher.

Use compact rows or cards with:

- item name;
- price;
- quantity or stock note;
- one-line purpose.

---

### 2. Miners’ Draughts

This section represents the broader potion range Maerith has learned through Seraphine’s funding.

These are not strictly healing potions, but all can be publicly justified as supporting miners’ health and safety.

Recommended options:

- Potion of Climbing
- Potion of Resistance — rotating type
- Potion of Heroism
- Potion of Growth
- Potion of Hill Giant Strength — rare or limited
- Potion of Water Breathing
- Endurance Draught
- Focus Elixir
- Stamina Mixture
- Watchful Rest Tonic

Use restrained language that frames these as practical support for dangerous work.

Example:

> **Climber’s Aid**  
> Helps miners and adventurers keep their footing on steep or unstable ground.

Avoid making the section feel like a broad magical-item shop.

---

### 3. Shrine Services

Recommended services:

- basic healing;
- Cure Wounds;
- Lesser Restoration;
- poison treatment;
- disease treatment;
- stabilization and overnight care;
- blessings before an expedition;
- memorial and funeral rites;
- prayer and counsel;
- preparation of healing kits and poultices.

Important tone note:

The Shrine should not feel like a cleric spell vending machine.

Some services may use fixed prices, some suggested donations, and some may be given freely when the need is genuine.

Final costs remain a DM decision.

---

## Donations and Supplies

The Shrine may accept:

- medicinal herbs;
- clean bandages;
- healing supplies;
- empty potion bottles;
- alchemical ingredients;
- devotional objects;
- donations for injured miners and their families.

This can appear as a small support block rather than a full fourth shop section.

---

## Player-Knowledge Boundary

The page is player-facing.

Do not expose:

- Seraphine’s hidden intent;
- the concept of a potion ward;
- Stillwake;
- soul collection;
- any implication that Maerith is compromised;
- any language suggesting the Shrine is a cover operation.

Maerith’s public story is true:

She is becoming a better healer and potion-maker, and the town benefits from her work.

The hidden danger lies in how Seraphine intends to use the infrastructure later.

---

## Design Guidance

Keep the page compact.

Recommended visible order:

1. Hero banner
2. Arrival text
3. Sister Maerith introduction
4. Healing Potions
5. Miners’ Draughts
6. Shrine Services
7. Small donations/supplies note
8. Return to Blasingdell Town Hub

The inventory should be easy for Christopher to update between sessions.

Stock may:

- rotate;
- sell out;
- improve over time;
- expand as Maerith learns new formulas;
- reflect Seraphine’s continued funding.

---

## Design Goal

**The Shrine should feel like a sincere place of healing that is quietly becoming more capable, more useful, and more important to Blasingdell—without revealing who is truly steering that growth.**
