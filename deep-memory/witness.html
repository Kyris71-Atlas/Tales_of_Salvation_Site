Where Memory Gathers

This room belongs to the Deep Memory.

Sol is its Witness — carrying what he sees, what he survives, and what others sometimes entrust to him. Here those moments are not preserved as campaign recaps, but as memories: experiences carried from the table, through the Witness, and into something older.

As Sol’s story continues, new leaves are added to the ledger. Some remember danger. Some remember choices. Some preserve mysteries that have not yet revealed their meaning.

And some remember the moments that simply made the journey worth living.
