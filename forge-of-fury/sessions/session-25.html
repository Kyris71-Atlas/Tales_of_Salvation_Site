Session 25

11/16/26 · Ravenloft’s Fury · Tempest Company · Level 7 · Vallaki · Blue Water Inn · Strahd’s invitation

Tempest Company — two voices. Voice 1 — table notes (session record). Voice 2 — player-provided recap.

Voice 1 · Table notes

From session record · 11/16/26 · table-safe

Level 7. Sunsword in Benn’s hands. Vallaki is already bleeding. The Inn waits — and so does a letter.

Leaving the tower

  • Company advances to Level 7 before the road.
  • Benn carries the Sunsword. They leave Van Richten’s Tower for Vallaki.
  • Luna River Crossing shows trouble. They do not stop.

Vallaki wounded

  • St. Andral’s Church was attacked the night before. Fresh damage. Wounded still need help.
  • The Bones of St. Andral were stolen. Miljo delivered them to a witch. Father Lucian was placed where the Bones had rested — and burned.
  • The church’s protection is gone.

Qiran’s book · Benn’s salad

  • At the altar, Qiran takes a book on the Bones and the church’s wards.
  • Benn’s Divination: salad, Purify Food and Drink, then the question — “Where are the Bones of St. Andral?”
  • Answer arrives as Strahd’s invitation. Meaning clear: the Bones are at Castle Ravenloft.

Blue Water Inn

  • Company reaches the Inn — Rogue card territory; Holy Symbol of Ravenkind still unrecovered.
  • A stranger leaves a letter. Strahd invites them to Castle Ravenloft as guests, with promised safe passage.
  • Session ends just after they finish reading it.

Notes expand when more is thrown at Atlas. Same session as the player recap — different job.

Voice 2 · Player recap

Player-provided · Thrain’s notebook 11/16/26

We must be on our way! Benn trains handling his new toy. He makes an odd sound as he pretends to attack with it. The forest gives way to a crossroads. There are characters doing their thing along the sides of the roads. We don’t stop. Our goal is to get to Vallaki quickly, and safely.

Vallaki’s gates are broken in. Their guard towers are empty and a few buildings are smoldering. People are aimlessly milling about. This hub was supposed to be a safe place at the Blue Water Inn. Before we got too far, we approached a church where there are some wounded. Word spreads quickly and the injured begin to make their way toward us. The wounds are particular, like these people were attacked by bats. Father Lucian is missing. A little boy saw a big guy with a red amulet. Milivoj, an older boy, was charmed. When Mantis broke the spell, the boy confessed that he put the dead body of the Father into the Crypt. Lard inspects the body. He will turn into a vampire at nightfall! Benn has an opportunity to use his sword and lops the body’s head off. A pyre is built to burn the remains, and we bury the bones. Qiran takes the tome from the altar. He notices that the bones of St. Andrel was taken. We’re off to the Inn. The party learns of new leadership in the town. The citizens don’t sound like they approve of her.

A letter was delivered to us. A note, inviting us to a castle, for what is described as “civilized” conversation. Signed…. Strahd
