The Pick & Lantern · Rooms, meals, strong drink, and stronger stories.

The Pick & Lantern

Warm lantern light spills across the common room as mugs strike tables and a dwarven chorus misses the same note together.

Tempest Company has heard these stories before. That does not mean the town has heard them correctly.

How stories travel in Blasingdell: Mapfoot tells what he saw. Tempest Company returns through the portal carrying scars, strange loot, and incomplete explanations. Someone hears part of it, someone improves it, and by morning the Pick & Lantern has done the rest.

What Blasingdell Says

Pull up a chair.
Hover a figure in the common room — click to hear who is talking.
The door returns you here.

Marta “Two-Tankard” Brindle says:Proprietor · Knows which version started where

“Tempest Company comes through that door carrying less food, more scars, and something nobody in town has seen before.”

“They eat, they drink, they answer three questions out of twenty, and by sunrise everyone in Blasingdell knows seven different versions of what happened.”

“Mine’s usually the closest.”

The Company · Reliable source

Garrick Lowbar says:Bar fixture · Alone with his mug

“The elf has two voices.”

“One belongs to Qiran. The other belongs to that knight in his sword.”

“I have never heard the second one myself, but my cousin’s friend heard him say, ‘Stand firm.’”

“Might have been Qiran. Might have been Marta telling someone to stop leaning on a table.”

Qiran · Thirdhand · Possibly furniture

Pell Nack says:Alehouse bookkeeper · Notices who checks their pockets

“Mantis put Rilkin Shadecloak to sleep. Just dropped him.”

“When Rilkin woke, he checked every pocket, started making a scene, then looked around and decided perhaps he had misplaced nothing after all.”

“Nobody knows what went missing.”

“That has not stopped anyone from naming it.”

Mantis & Rilkin · Uncertain ending

Rufflin Coalhand says:Retired shift boss · Northwest wall

“Mantis does the talking, sure. He’s the Voice of Tempest Company.”

“But watch Bearblood before the little storm-mage answers. Thrain’s already checked the doors, counted the trouble, and decided which road gets everyone home.”

“He leads from the rear. Smart place to stand when everyone in front keeps finding monsters.”

Thrain · Mostly true

Brann Copperkettle says:Market regular · Tracks Lady Vellbrandt’s tone

“Lady Vellbrandt never boasts about them. That’s how you know she’s impressed.”

“She just mentions, very casually, that Tempest Company recovered a shipment, cleared a road, rescued some miners, or survived something nobody else would enter.”

“Then she leaves before anyone can ask questions.”

“By supper, the story has doubled.”

Lady Vellbrandt · Useful half-truths

Hollis Chartwick says:Night clerk · Collects maps he will never walk

“Mapfoot was in last week—Brennar Tallowick, the little cartographer who runs with Tempest Company.”

“Said Qiran and Thrain actually listened when he described what he saw. Properly listened. He said you remember that when you are underground and everyone else is deciding whether the cartographer counts as an adventurer.”

“And the crossbow? Well, yes. He has finished off a few creatures. Happens all the time—so he claims.”

Mapfoot · Eyewitness · Secondhand at the inn

Edd Bellweather says:Shrine regular · Evening bell volunteer

“Sister Maerith includes Tempest Company in her daily blessings.”

“Every morning.”

“Health for the wounded. Courage for those below. Wisdom enough to know when to return.”

“She says the last part a little louder.”

The Shrine · Well known

Bilt Flintcap says:Greytooth miner · Survived the Cracked Vein

“Twenty-six of us went down at once. Not staggered. Not one after another. One breath we were working, and the next—nothing.”

“Some woke where they fell. Some dreamed. Some still disagree about what they remember.”

“Tempest Company went below while everyone else was deciding how far away to stand.”

The Cracked Vein · Firsthand

Benn · Possibly both

“Mantis looked around and said, ‘We’re fine.’”

“Nobody who says ‘we’re fine’ that quickly has ever been fine.”
